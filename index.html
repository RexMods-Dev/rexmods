Background

How it started

The Forza Styled Speedometer project originally started in 2021. At the time there were a lot of requests from the community for a Forza Horizon style speedometer in GTA V, and that was enough motivation to give it a try.

One of the main reasons the project got off the ground was JulioNIB's speedometer tutorial and the help he provided. Without that it probably wouldn't have gone anywhere.

The original version was eventually abandoned due to texture issues and other technical limitations. The project sat unfinished for a while before I decided to come back to it.

When I returned, I restarted everything from scratch. After a lot of redesigning, texturing and testing, the project became what it is today — the current Forza Styled Speedometer.
